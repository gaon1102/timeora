How to use
  1. Turn the hours, minutes and seconds dials by scrolling (one notch changes one number), swiping, clicking a number or typing digits, or press a preset (1, 5, 10, 15, 25, 30 or 60 minutes). Presets start right away.
  2. Press Start. The ring and the numbers show the time left, and the countdown also appears in the browser tab title so you can follow it from another tab.
  3. Press Pause to hold the countdown and Resume to continue. Press Cancel to clear it.
  4. When time is up, a sound plays until you press Stop.

Good to know

  • Keep this tab open until the timer finishes. Closing it or putting the device to sleep will stop the timer from ringing.
  • Pressing Start also turns the sound on, because browsers only allow sound after you interact with the page.
  • Timers you start are listed under “Recently used” (up to 10, newest first). Press one to start it again.

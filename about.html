About Timeora

Every second, precisely.

Timeora is a collection of free online time tools designed to help you check and manage time easily.

No complicated setup. Just open a tool and get started.

What We Offer

World Clock

Check the current time in cities around the world and compare different time zones.

Alarm Clock

Set alarms for the moments that matter, with sound and repeat-day options.

Timer

Use a simple countdown timer for studying, cooking, exercise and everyday tasks.

Stopwatch

Measure elapsed time and keep track of lap times whenever you need to.

Our Approach

We believe useful tools should be simple, accessible and easy to use.

Timeora lets you get started without creating an account, with a focus on clear design and straightforward controls.

Because checking the time should be simple.
